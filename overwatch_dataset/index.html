Overwatch Depth Dataset

An Outdoor RGB–Depth Dataset for Monocular Depth Estimation

The Overwatch Depth Dataset contains pixel-aligned RGB images and dense depth maps captured from diverse synthetic outdoor environments. Streets, buildings, vegetation, open spaces, and long-range sky regions provide varied geometry for training and evaluating monocular depth estimation models.

  • Paired RGB and depth images are provided at a preview resolution of 1024 × 576.
  • Depth is stored on a shared 8-bit dataset scale: black represents nearer geometry and white represents farther geometry. The images are not independently min–max normalized per frame.
  • The scale is relative and its conversion to meters is not available. The dataset is therefore intended for relative-depth training and scale-aligned evaluation rather than reporting metric errors in meters.
  • Spectral color is used only for visualization and does not change the underlying grayscale depth values.
  • Five environments form the training split, while Midtown is reserved for testing.
